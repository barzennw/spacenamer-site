What happens next

Install it

Unzip, drag SpaceNamer into Applications, open it. It is notarized, so macOS lets it straight through — no right-click tricks.

It asks for a license

SpaceNamer is paid software with no trial. On first launch its menu offers Buy SpaceNamer… and Enter License…, and nothing else works until a key is in.
