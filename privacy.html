Summary

SpaceNamer is a menu bar app that names your Mac’s desktops. It has no account system, collects no analytics and contains no tracking of any kind. The names and emoji you choose are stored on your own Mac and are never transmitted anywhere. The single exception is license activation, described below.

What stays on your Mac

All of the following is stored locally in SpaceNamer’s preferences and never sent anywhere:

  • The names and emoji you assign to each desktop.
  • The identifiers macOS uses for your desktops and displays, which are what those names are attached to.
  • Your preference for launching at login.
  • Your license key and the activation record for this Mac, after activation.

Deleting the app and its preferences removes all of it. Nothing is synced between your Macs, which is why a second Mac starts with default names.

What leaves your Mac

SpaceNamer makes network requests for exactly one purpose: checking that your license is valid. This happens when you activate a key, when you remove it, and during a quiet revalidation roughly every two weeks. Those requests go to our licensing provider, Kelviq, and contain:

  • Your license key.
  • An activation identifier that stands for this Mac’s seat, so a seat can later be freed.
  • The network metadata any web request carries, such as your IP address.

Your desktop names, your emoji, the apps you run and anything on your screen are not part of these requests. If the network is unavailable the app keeps working offline.

Payment

Purchases are handled by Kelviq, who are the merchant of record for every sale — they take the payment, handle tax, and manage refunds and order enquiries. We never see or store your card details. We do receive the email address you buy with, so we can send your license key and reply to support.

What is never collected

  • No analytics, telemetry or usage statistics.
  • No crash reports sent automatically.
  • No screen contents — the app never requests Screen Recording permission.
  • No keystrokes — the app never requests Accessibility permission.
  • No list of your apps, files, windows or browsing.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no data sold or shared with advertisers.
