When you should not buy SpaceNamer

Two or three desktops

If you can hold your layout in your head, macOS on its own is fine. This tool earns its keep at around five desktops.

A three-letter label is enough

If EML and DEV tell you everything you need, Spaceman is free and does that well. Take the free one.

You never switch from the menu bar

If keyboard shortcuts already take you everywhere, you are paying mostly for the label.

What you are paying for

  • Real names, not abbreviations. “Harbor & Pine” instead of H&P, with an emoji in front of it.
  • One-click switching that uses no permissions. Clicking a name hands focus to a window on that desktop and macOS does its own switch. Nothing fakes keystrokes, so nothing needs Accessibility.
  • Per-display lists. Two monitors means two sets of desktops, listed separately with a checkmark on each.
  • Maintenance. Each new macOS release is tested the week it ships, and the result is published on the compatibility page.

About the free tools. Spaceman and WhichSpace are open-source projects maintained by other people, and they can change at any time. The notes above describe them as of the date in the table — check the projects themselves before deciding.
